Common Questions

We know you have questions.
Here are the honest answers.

Heirlo is not a law firm. We believe in being clear about what this document is — and what it isn’t.

Is this a will? +

No. Heirlo generates a Personal Property Memorandum (PPM) — a separate document type recognized under the Uniform Probate Code. It supplements your will, or stands on its own for personal property. It doesn’t replace a will.

Is it legally binding? +

Personal Property Memorandums are legally recognized in most US states for tangible personal property when certain conditions are met. Heirlo is built to meet those conditions. We still recommend consulting an estate attorney for your full situation.

Does the recipient have to sign anything? +

No. Recipients receive an email and click to acknowledge your wishes. This creates a timestamped record — but it’s not a legal signature. The acknowledgment strengthens your document; the document is what carries legal weight.

What does it cost? +

Heirlo is free during the beta. We’ll announce pricing before we leave beta, and everyone on the waitlist will receive advance notice and a meaningful discount.

What happens to my data? +

Your data is encrypted and stored securely. We never sell or share your personal information. You can export or delete your data at any time.

How is this different from just writing it down? +

Heirlo creates a structured, timestamped record with photos, recipient acknowledgments, and properly formatted legal language. A handwritten note in a drawer can’t provide that paper trail — and it can’t be signed or notarized.
